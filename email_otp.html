How It Works

This tool demonstrates how OTPs sent via email can be intercepted through various methods:

SMTP/IMAP Proxying

Intercepts email traffic by acting as a proxy between the user and email servers.

Network Interception

Uses MiTM attacks to capture unencrypted email communications on unsecured networks.

Email Parsing

Extracts OTPs from emails using pattern recognition and content analysis.

Research Purpose Only: This tool is for educational and research purposes to understand email OTP security vulnerabilities and implement better protection mechanisms.
Email OTP Security Recommendations
  • Use Encrypted Connections: Always use TLS/SSL for email transmissions
  • Implement SPF, DKIM, and DMARC: Protect against email spoofing
  • Unique OTP Formats: Avoid using common patterns that are easy to detect
  • Multi-Factor Authentication: Use additional verification methods beyond email
  • Limited Validity Period: Set short expiration times for OTPs
  • Email Encryption: Consider end-to-end encrypted email for sensitive information
